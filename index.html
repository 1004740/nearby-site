    Set up in under a minute

    No passwords, and you choose how far afield to look.

    1. Sign in

      With Apple, Google or a code sent to your email.

    2. Enter your postcode

      Nearby finds your ward and council and starts pulling in what's happening around you.

    3. Read, filter, post

      Look anywhere from half a kilometre to five, and share things with your neighbours.

    Built for neighbours, not for trolls

    Your full postcode stays private

    Other people only ever see the first half, like M1. Posts are placed about 100 m from your home, never on it.

    Real locals only

    Your postcode is checked before you can post, so outsiders can't stir up your street.

    Moderated from day one

    Reports go to a real person. Posts reported by several neighbours are hidden until they're checked.

    Leave whenever you like

    Delete your account in the app and your posts and postcode go with it.
