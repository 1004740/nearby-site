This notice covers this website: the weekly Letter by email, the app waitlist, and the shared story pages. The Dear Borough app will have its own privacy notice.

Who we are

Dear Borough is run by [your name or company name, and address]. We are the controller of your personal data. Contact us at [email address] with any question about your data.

The weekly Letter by email

What we collect:

  • Your email address.
  • A record of your consent: that you ticked the box, the wording version (for example letter-2026-11), the time, and the page you signed up on.
  • Whether you have confirmed your address or unsubscribed, and when.
  • To stop abuse of the sign-up form, a scrambled (hashed) form of your email address and of your IP address, made with a secret key. We can't turn it back into the original, but it is still personal data. We delete it within 24 hours of the sign-up request, and at the latest after 7 days.

We use it to send you the Letter once a week and one email to confirm your address. Our lawful basis is your consent. You confirm by clicking the link in the first email, and nothing more is sent until you do. Every Letter has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing takes effect straight away.

The app waitlist

  • Your email address.
  • The first half of your postcode (for example RM1), if you give it. We never store the full postcode.
  • That you agreed to be emailed, and when.

We use your email address to tell you when Dear Borough opens to you, and the first half of your postcode to count sign-ups by area. Our lawful basis is your consent, given by ticking the box on the form.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores the Letter list and the waitlist. [Confirm the Supabase project region. The plan is London; if it is not, say where and what safeguard covers transfers outside the UK.]
  • Resend sends the confirmation email and the Letter, so it receives your email address. Resend is based in the United States. [Name the safeguard for that transfer, for example the UK-US data bridge or the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses, as for Supabase above.]
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and runs Turnstile, the check on the Letter form that tells people from bots. Cloudflare handles technical information about your visit to do this. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

We don't sell your details or share them with advertisers.

Story pages

When someone shares a link to a story, consultation or event, the page shows public information from the source named on it. We don't collect personal data from reading these pages.

How long we keep it

The Letter:

  • Your email address and consent record are kept while you are subscribed.
  • If you sign up but never confirm, we delete your details 30 days after we send the confirmation email.
  • If you unsubscribe, we delete your details, including your address at Resend, 30 days after you unsubscribe. We keep them for that long so that your unsubscribe is respected and we can show when it happened.
  • The scrambled abuse-prevention data is deleted as described above.

We keep waitlist details until Dear Borough opens to you and we've emailed you, or until you ask us to remove them. [Add a maximum period for the waitlist, for example 2 years.]

Your rights

You can withdraw consent, unsubscribe, or ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing [email address]. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and fonts

Our pages set no cookies and use no analytics. They use fonts already on your device, so nothing is loaded from a font service. The Turnstile check on the Letter form is run by Cloudflare.
