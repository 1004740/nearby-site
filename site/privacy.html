Privacy notice: waitlist

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to your details when you join the Dear Borough waitlist on this website. It covers the waitlist only. The app has its own privacy notice, which will be published before the app is available.

Who is responsible

Dear Borough is run by Kudzai Madziya, who is responsible for your details (the data controller). Dear Borough is independent and is not run by any council.

Questions about your details: hello@dearborough.london

What is collected

  • Your email address
  • The London borough you chose
  • The date you joined, and a record that you ticked the box to agree

What it is used for

To send you an email when Dear Borough reaches your borough. It is not used for anything else, it is not sold, and it is not shared for marketing.

The legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing hello@dearborough.london, and your details will be deleted.

Where it is kept

Your details are stored in a database run by Supabase, on servers in London (eu-west-2). This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Neither company uses your details for its own purposes.

How long it is kept

Until your borough is added and the email has been sent, or until you ask for it to be deleted. After the email is sent, your details are deleted within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, by emailing hello@dearborough.london. A reply is sent within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your details are handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.
